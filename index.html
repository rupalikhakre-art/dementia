IDEA TITLE

Proposed Solution: Smriti Setu ("The Memory Bridge")

The Concept

An AI-powered, culturally localized cognitive maintenance platform for elderly dementia patients in the North Eastern Region.

It utilizes regional motifs (like Assam tea gardens) and native voice guidance for offline digital therapy.

Addressing the Problem

Standard apps use confusing Western icons. Smriti Setu relies on familiar, low-stress games to stimulate memory.

It passively tracks reaction times, giving caregivers early warnings of cognitive decline without forced clinical tests.

Innovation

  • No "Wrong" Answers: Adaptive games prevent patient anxiety.
  • 100% Offline: Local storage (IndexedDB) with deferred batch-syncing.
  • Native Voice: Talks in local languages without internet APIs.

FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY

Analysis of Feasibility

Smriti Setu is optimized for low-cost, subsidized Android tablets (1GB RAM). Relying exclusively on native browser APIs eliminates recurring cloud costs, ensuring economic sustainability for rural healthcare initiatives.

Challenges / Risks Strategies for Mitigation
Erratic 2G/Cellular Connectivity: Poor network in hilly terrains. Offline Engine batches data locally (IndexedDB), syncing only when stable connection is caught.
Low Tech-Literacy: Difficulties navigating standard smartphone UIs. Single-tap routine check-cards and local voice guidance replace complex menus.
Differentiating Decline: Hard to separate bad days from neurological drop. "Sundowning Alerts" cross-reference daily latency trends to flag distinct clinical anomalies.
